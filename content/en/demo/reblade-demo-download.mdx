## Tips for Getting More Out of Your First Runs

The **ReBlade demo download** gives you limited time with limited tools, so approach it like a lab session rather than a race.

1. **Spend your first run learning enemy tells.** Do not worry about winning. Watch the wind-up animations and note which attacks are punishable.
2. **Test one weapon type per run.** Switching constantly makes it hard to judge whether a build is working.
3. **Read chip and augment text carefully.** Roguelite synergies are usually hidden in the wording, not in the raw numbers.
4. **Prioritize defense early.** Timing-based combat punishes greed, and survival gives you more chances to learn patterns.
5. **Fight the same beast repeatedly.** Pattern recognition beats raw damage in this style of game.
6. **Wishlist the full game.** It is the only reliable way to get notified when the release date is announced.

Player experience suggests that the demo's toughest moments are the ones worth replaying. A fight that feels unfair on the first attempt usually has a tell you have not learned yet.
