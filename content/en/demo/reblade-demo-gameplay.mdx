## The Core Loop You'll Feel in ReBlade Demo Gameplay

The full game is built on a five-beat loop, and even a short session should give you a feel for most of it.

### Move, touch, cut

There is no attack input. Positioning is everything. Your skill expression comes from predicting where enemies will cluster and dragging the blade through them rather than chasing individual targets.

### Pick an Awakening

During combat you're offered Awakening effects, and you choose one. These aren't passive stat bumps — the store page is explicit that the effect you pick changes *how* your sword attacks and how it develops. That makes Awakenings the closest thing the game has to a build-defining decision.

### Upgrade, then Raid

Rewards earned in fights go back into your sword. Once it's strong enough, you step into a Raid, fight the boss, and move on to the next battle.

| Step | What you do | What to test in the demo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Steer | Move the mouse; the sword follows | Whether tracking feels smooth at your current sensitivity |
| 2. Cut | Touching an enemy attacks automatically | Whether hit detection feels fair or finicky |
| 3. Choose | Pick one Awakening from the options offered | Whether the choices feel meaningfully different |
| 4. Upgrade | Spend combat rewards on your sword | Whether the progression pace holds your attention |
| 5. Raid | Enter a boss encounter when you're strong enough | Whether the difficulty ramp feels earned |

If you only have fifteen minutes, spend it on steps 2 and 3. Those are the two places where a demo either wins you over or loses you.

## Practical Tips for Better ReBlade Demo Gameplay

Treat the demo as a lab, not a race. A few habits make the short session far more informative.

- **Fix your sensitivity first.** Spend two minutes dialing in mouse DPI before you judge the combat. Most "the controls feel off" complaints in cursor-driven games trace back to sensitivity, not design.
- **Lead your targets.** Because attacks trigger on contact, dragging the blade *through* a group beats chasing one enemy at a time.
- **Read every Awakening.** They change how the sword behaves, not just how hard it hits. Skipping the text defeats the point of the system.
- **Don't hoard upgrades.** Spending rewards early tells you more about the progression curve than sitting on currency.
- **Test on the hardware you'll actually use.** If you plan to play on Steam Deck, verify the demo there rather than on a desktop.

| --- | --- | --- |
| Sensitivity setup | Everything downstream depends on it | 2 minutes |
| Enemy grouping practice | Fastest way to feel the skill ceiling | 5 minutes |
| One full Awakening cycle | Shows how builds diverge | 5 minutes |
| A Raid attempt, if reachable | Reveals the difficulty curve | Variable |
