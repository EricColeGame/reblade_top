## Tips for Getting More Out of the ReBlade Steam Next Fest Demo

A free demo is easy to bounce off in five minutes and forget. A little structure gets you a much better read on whether the game is for you.

- **Play the first run blind.** Resist the urge to look up builds before your initial attempt. Your gut reaction to the combat feel is the most valuable data point you will get.
- **Give it at least three runs.** Action-roguelikes often feel thin on run one and click on run three, once the upgrade system has had room to breathe.
- **Test two very different builds.** If the demo offers branching upgrades, push one run toward offense and another toward defense or mobility.
- **Watch the frame rate, not just the art.** Performance in a demo is a reasonable proxy for performance at launch, especially in combat-heavy scenes.
- **Try the controls on your actual setup.** If you plan to play with a controller, do not evaluate the game with mouse and keyboard.
- **Capture one clip of combat.** Watching a 15-second recording later is the clearest way to judge whether the hit feedback actually lands.
- **Leave feedback where the developers will see it.** Steam forums and the official Discord are the channels studios actually read during a festival.
- **Wishlist before you close the client.** This is the single highest-value action you can take for a small studio.

| Priority | Action | Time Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High | Wishlist the game | 5 seconds |
| High | Complete three runs | 30–90 minutes |
| Medium | Test two build paths | 20 minutes |
| Medium | Post feedback in Steam forums | 5 minutes |
| Low | Rewatch the trailer for details you missed | 2 minutes |

One more tip specific to festival demos: check whether the developers are streaming during the event. Next Fest includes developer livestreams, and a short Q&A often answers the save-carryover and release-date questions faster than any store page.
