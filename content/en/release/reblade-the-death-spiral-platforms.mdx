## Why Everyone Is Asking About ReBlade: The Death Spiral Platforms

| Setup item | Official guidance | Why it matters |

The v0.4.44 patch, pushed during Steam Next Fest, added a genuinely useful quality-of-life fix: if you quit mid-run, you can now return to the combat room instead of losing your progress. That matters more than it sounds, because roguelike runs are long and life interrupts.

Controller support remains a work in progress. The team explicitly recommends an Xbox pad for the best experience, which is a candid admission that other input devices may behave inconsistently. If you are a keyboard-and-mouse loyalist, expect to do some remapping.

## FAQ

**Is the ReBlade: The Death Spiral platforms lineup really limited to PC?**

Officially, yes. Every piece of promotional material points to Steam on Windows PC, and no console or mobile version has been announced. Additional ReBlade: The Death Spiral platforms could appear after launch, but nothing has been confirmed.

**How long can I play the demo?**

The official announcement lists October 19 through November 10, timed to Steam Next Fest. Whether the demo returns after that window has not been stated, so treat those dates as your deadline.

**Will my demo save carry over to the full game?**

No. The developers have said underlying systems will change before release, so demo save data will not transfer. Your files live in the ChillyRoom folder under AppData\LocalLow if you want to back them up or attach them to a bug report.

**Do I need a controller to enjoy the demo?**

Not strictly, but the team recommends an Xbox controller while compatibility work continues. Keyboard and mouse works, though you may want to remap keys before a long session.
